# Simplification

Inspect the concrete artifact. Identify duplication, dead paths, unnecessary abstraction, repeated instructions, and avoidable coordination. Explain the exact deletion or consolidation and its effect on behavior. Preserve the minimum complete capability. Escalate changes to module boundaries, contracts, or ownership to the architecture skill. Do not manufacture criticism.
