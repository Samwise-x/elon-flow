# Source lineage

Elon-Flow is an independent, rewritten engineering workflow assembled from user-selected source material and original synthesis.

Its design was informed by:

- the project's supplied reconstruction of Elon Musk's engineering and operating philosophy;
- the public `backnotprop/pstack` repository and its upstream pstack lineage;
- the installed Ponytail engineering skill set;
- the installed Matt Pocock engineering skill set.

The plugin intentionally does not copy those systems wholesale. It extracts and recomposes mechanisms behind a separate ownership and routing model.

Before any public distribution, verify the license and attribution requirements of every source whose wording or implementation detail is incorporated. Keep source-derived wording paraphrased unless its license and attribution terms are explicitly satisfied.
